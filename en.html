How to jump

One jump, five moments

Easy to pick up, hard to master. Every jump lands somewhere else – it's all about reflexes and feel.

  1. 1

    Green light

    You sit on the bar. Red, yellow, green – the clock counts down 10 seconds. Press A and go.

  2. 2

    Take-off

    A right before the edge. The game times it and tells you if you were early. No take-off, no flight.

  3. 3

    Flight

    Use the stick to hold your position in the green. In competitions a line on the landing hill shows what you need to beat the leader.

  4. 4

    Telemark

    When the ring turns green, press B and A together. One button gives you a safe two-footed landing.

  5. 5

    Marks

    Five judges, points for distance, style, wind and gate. The game shows what cost you points.

Leader line

In competitions a line on the landing hill shows what you need to take the lead. Fly past it and the metres turn yellow.

What cost you points

Next to the marks, a yellow line says what lost you points: take-off, flight or landing.

Take turns

Up to 4 people on one console. Everyone enters a name and jumps in turn – just like in a real competition.

Wind and gate

The wind changes during the whole jump, and a gauge shows if it's a headwind or a tailwind. In competitions the jury moves the gate.

Jump for your country

In the team event you pick one of 14 countries. The list shows each flag and squad, and you take the place of the weakest jumper.

Replays

Slow motion right after the jump. R1 saves the best ones for later.
